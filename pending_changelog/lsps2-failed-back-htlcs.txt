# API Updates

 * `LSPS2ServiceHandler::htlc_handling_failed` now drops a held HTLC that LDK failed back, as
   named by the `intercept_id` of `HTLCHandlingFailureType::InvalidForward`, from its buy's
   payment queue. Previously the HTLC stayed queued and still counted toward the buy's payment.

 * `LSPS2ServiceHandler::channel_ready` no longer returns an error for a buy whose held payment
   was failed back before its channel became ready. A new channel's buy forwards the next payment
   that can be forwarded as the fee payment; a buy whose splice locked is reset, as by
   `splice_failed`, for its payer to pay again.
