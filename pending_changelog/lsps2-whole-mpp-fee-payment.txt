# Bug Fixes

 * In "MPP+fixed-invoice" mode, an LSPS2 service no longer forwards part of a payment as the fee
   payment: neither a partial payment queued ahead of the one that triggered the channel open,
   nor the first part of a payment arriving after a fee payment failed. Only a payment whose
   parts total `payment_size_msat` is forwarded, and a partial one is held, as before the open.
